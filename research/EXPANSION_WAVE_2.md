# Felicity — Expansion wave 2: twenty additional upgrade directions

**Research date:** October 25, 2026. **Vehicle:** Canadian-built June 2003, 2004 U.S. Corolla LE, 1ZZ-FE/A245E. **Scope:** upgrades vs **healthy OEM**, not restoration of damaged parts. Discovery is unlimited in scale; immediate shopping still favors inexpensive/easy but does **not restrict** what we investigate.

**This is a screening pass, not a list of 20 verified improvements.** Each new research idea has a stable ID (163–182), at least one traceable source, benefit mechanism, install burden, disadvantages, and its own uncertainty. [Full value and fitment matrix](../database/upgrade_value_matrix.csv).

## Evidence maturity and immediate priorities

- **Credible candidate product or fuel choice**: 163–166, 176–178, 181. Some are inexpensive but *none* has a controlled incremental Corolla MPG result.
- **Vehicle/interface and lifecycle research needed**: 167–175, 179–180, 182. Some are unlikely to be economical even if technically possible.
- **Higher value discovery for NY climate**: genuine 1ZZ block heater fitment and charge maintenance; **do not claim block heating saves gas without calculating electrical input, weather, trip lengths and idle time**.
- **Healthy original technical baseline correction**: reconcile Toyota chart oil filter YZZF2 and current YZZN1 number through EPC/supersession, not a worn-part comparison.
- **No license to modify steering, refrigerant, coolant or battery distribution unsafely.**

This expansion does **not** introduce a new count ceiling at 182. The next evidence batch should search across additional supplier families and test standards, distinguish SKU alternatives from truly new ideas, and update all linked CSV records.
