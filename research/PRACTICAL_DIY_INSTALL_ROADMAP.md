## Stage 3: Later, higher-impact investments

- **Tires/wheel profile**: current photographed 215/45R18 has ~97mm sidewall vs OEM 195/65R15 ~127mm; wheel conversion could improve ride, but tire construction, pressures, wheels and costs matter; never claim guaranteed result. Price/geometry not yet validated.
- **Ride / chassis / steering**: evaluate when symptoms, intended feel, and cost are established. Replacing worn parts restores baseline, not a new upgrade; stiffer components can worsen comfort.
- **More extensive sound insulation**: only after localized material trial and noise baseline; respect drainage, restraint and airbag regions.

## Decision rules

1. Start with desired driver experience, not upgrade count. Prioritize road-trip comfort, efficiency and long-term reliability.
2. Every item lists total purchase cost, install steps, exact early-build fitment, healthy-OEM advantage and tradeoffs before entering Stage 1.
3. No large install batch or expensive purchase just because a candidate exists.
4. Keep the [193-candidate matrix](../database/upgrade_value_matrix.csv) as background research, not a buying list.
5. Record before/after tests when possible (cabin noise same speed/road/pressure; temp under same sun exposure); phone dB values are comparative, not calibrated proof.
