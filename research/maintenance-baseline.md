## Factory interval evidence
Toyota's *2004 Corolla and Corolla Matrix Scheduled Maintenance Guide*, General Maintenance Information p.15, distinguishes **5,000-mile/4-month** intervals for dusty/unpaved roads, towing/roof carrier or repeated under-5-mile drives below freezing, vs **7,500-mile/6-month** intervals otherwise. The calendar period matters as well as mileage.

The guide's Corolla entries include regular engine oil/filter service, tire rotation, brakes/suspension checks, and periodic air/cabin filters. At **90,000 miles**, it lists **engine coolant replacement**, engine air filter, oil/filter and multiple chassis/fluid inspections. At **120,000 miles**, spark-plug replacement is listed. The 2004 Toyota repair reference independently says spark plugs every 120,000 miles. Confirm completed work and driving conditions before marking overdue.

## Order of operations (condition-based, NOT an assertion of overdue services)
1. **Before driving:** inspect dipstick oil level. A previously reported nearly-empty level needs clarification. If oil-pressure warning persists, **stop engine** and investigate; a dipstick check is not a pressure test.
2. **Safety:** brake hydraulics/lines/rotors or drums, tire age/tread/pressure, suspension mounts and severe corrosion, all lighting, belts, warning lamps and any open VIN recalls.
3. **Engine baseline:** date/mileage of last engine oil/filter, intake/cabin filter, fuel and coolant leaks, cooling performance, accessory belt wear, air-filter housing condition.
4. **Cooling:** documented replacement at 90k? If unknown, identify currently used coolant, contamination and leak risks; use correct chemistry and safe cold-engine procedures.
5. **Automatic transmission:** Toyota owner's manual specifies **Toyota ATF Type T-IV**, up to **3.0 L (3.2 US qt) on drain/refill**; this is NOT total dry capacity or an instruction for a flush. Record leaks, shift behavior and ATF history. Its replacement guidance depends on listed special operating conditions.
6. **Ignition:** verify service history; factory spark plugs DENSO SK16R11 or NGK IFR5A11; factory service interval 120k miles, not automatically due simply because vehicle is >20 years old.
7. **Baseline measurements:** OBD-II codes with freeze frame; battery resting/cranking/charging voltage; fuel economy and oil usage per 1,000 mi; fluid levels; observations documented before repairs.
