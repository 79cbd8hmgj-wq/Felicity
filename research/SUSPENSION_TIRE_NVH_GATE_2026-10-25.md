## Incremental engineering experiments (low-cost, no unnecessary purchases)

### A. Tire/wheel isolation baseline
- Record all four make/model, complete sidewall load and speed designation, tire age/DOT, measured **cold** PSI and tread depths, wheel stamped size and offset, spacers, wheel/lug seat type and mass only if safely removable.
- Document handling on repeated same-route asphalt joints, sharp-edged patches, coarse pavement and highway segments. Use 3 repeated passes under comparable temperature, load and tire conditions; record phone location fixed in a secure holder.
- Inspect tires for bulges/abnormal wear and contact evidence before road testing. **Do not intentionally lower pressures to improve ride.** Use car placard and tire/wheel provider guidance for non-OE configurations.
- Compare proposed 15in assembly on cost, availability, safe fit, tire independent wet/braking performance, load rating, ride and vibration, not sidewall alone.

### B. Suspension source/path separation
- **Steering shake at speed:** check tire/wheel balance, runout, irregular tread wear, wheel centering and torque first; do not treat damping mats as solution.
- **Single-wheel sharp impact and clunks:** document corner, speed, compression/rebound symptom and links/ball joints/mounts/bump stops; inspect professionally if loose or unsafe.
- **Float/secondary bounce:** assess worn dampers and springs as **restoration** before seeking aftermarket tuning.
- **Coarse-pavement tire roar:** confirm source with consistent speed and route before trunk damping/absorption trial; trunk resonance may not be controlling source.
- **Highway hiss:** confirm weatherstrips, glass closure and door alignment, then wind-direction sensitivity. Do not stuff seals in ways that hinder safe door closure or introduce leaks.
- Do not tighten suspension bushings with wheels hanging if service procedure calls for normal ride-height tightening; validate factory torque procedures against June-specific service information.

### C. Acoustic material experiment gate
- For **panel resonance**, tap-test noncritical accessible trunk panels, inspect existing damping and install a **small** reversible-layout / minimized-coverage trial before blanket treatment. Constrained-layer damping and porous absorption are different mechanisms.
- For **airborne middle/high-frequency sound**, 3M SM600L may be useful with sufficient cavity clearance; record actual measured depth and allow normal trim installation. Do not place near SRS/seatbelt assemblies, drains, electronics heat sources, steering or moving controls.
- Record before-and-after phone audio/dBA as **relative uncalibrated comparisons**, repeat same road, speed and fixed microphone position; avoid interpreting small changes within device and traffic variability as proof.
