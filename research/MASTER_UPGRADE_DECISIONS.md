**Important:** This index is a *decision overlay on preliminary research*, **not 150 independently re-verified performance evaluations**. It is designed to make known/unknown evidence clear, not to invent quantitative gains. No row may be called a confirmed vehicle-specific improvement on manufacturer's claimed fitment alone.

## Standard format for a future recommendation

- **Primary goal:** fuel gallons/year, mechanical efficiency, electrical watts, filtration quality, thermal burden, grip/handling, or added function.
- **Healthy Toyota baseline and owner's current modified system** (separate references where they differ).
- **Delta:** controlled same-car measurement when available; otherwise manufacturer or bench specification and state that vehicle delta remains unknown.
- **Installation:** tool list, access, time (known or unknown), whether outside owner DIY constraints.
- **Incremental cost** versus retaining functioning factory equipment; full price including shipping and tools when available.
- **Risk and payback:** legal, heat, flow, tire traction, durability and expected dollar savings, not automatic 'improvement'.
- **Verdict:** conditional useful purchase / replacement at service / research-only / outside current budget.

This report is the **decision gateway**; individual research passes remain background evidence only. Do not accidentally promote product fitment statuses or quantify untested fuel savings.
