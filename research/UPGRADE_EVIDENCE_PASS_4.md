# Upgrade evidence pass 4 — cost and install corrections (2026-10-25)

**Baseline:** healthy OEM 2004 Corolla LE / 1ZZ-FE / A245E. Sources checked again October 25, 2026. No unsupported horsepower or stopping-distance gains.

## Progress 62.2150 rear anti-roll bar — primary instructions verified directly
- Maker installation sheet (Rev B, Aug 9, 2022): https://www.progressauto.com/ecom/download/62.2150.pdf
- Covers 2001–08 Corolla/Matrix; actual manufacturer's instructions call for qualified licensed suspension mechanic, jack stands, removal/reuse of two beam bolts, new 24mm bar, and torque **150 ft-lb**, then settle suspension and periodically check tightness. Do **not** assume simple drive-on/no-lift installation.
- Maker application guide identifies 2003–08 Corolla 62.2150, 24 mm: https://progressauto.com/ApplicationGuideExcelComplete2-9-15-A.pdf
- Evasive listing: **$214.20** on research date, product shown for 2003–08 Corolla/Matrix: https://www.evasivemotorsports.com/store/product/progress-technology-antisway-bar-rear-24mm-toyota-matrixcorolla-03-08/
- Healthy-OE incremental claim: increased rear roll stiffness and changed balance; no independent controlled same-car quantitative driving result. Actual handling can worsen under some conditions, particularly reduced understeer/increased oversteer. Conditional meaningful-handling recommendation.

## MagnaFlow 15807 — current seller stock vs manufacturer stock differs
- Etrailer listing **$817.77 and in stock** on retrieval, explicitly 2003–06 Corolla 1.8L application: https://www.etrailer.com/Exhaust-Systems/MagnaFlow/MF15807.html
- Prior manufacturer page **$830 and out of stock**: https://www.magnaflow.com/products/15807-magnaflow-2003-2006-toyota-corolla-1-8l-street-series-cat-back-performance-exhaust-system-15807
- Retailer presents an **OEM-vs-MagnaFlow dyno graphic**, but page does not give test vehicle configuration, numeric dyno pairs, conditions, or independently attributable Corolla 1ZZ-FE gain. The presence of a graph isn't automatically proof of an exact-application gain.
- Manufacturer/JEGS installation instructions say **cut stock exhaust ahead of resonator**: https://www.jegs.com/InstallationInstructions/600/642/642-15807_i.pdf. This is not a simple no-modification bolt-on. Exhaust cutting is out of present preferred easy/moderate *without fabrication/cutting* category.
- **Decision:** defer as $818+ purchase for power without verified incremental hp. Do not describe seller availability as universally out of stock.
