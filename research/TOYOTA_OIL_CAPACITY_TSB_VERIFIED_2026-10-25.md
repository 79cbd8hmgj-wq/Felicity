**Evidence improvement over earlier pass:** recovered full two-page US Toyota technical service bulletin, dated July 15 2008. No physical inspection or service has occurred.

## Factory bulletin specification (now verified)

Source: https://device.report/m/5ae76545e046a61ccd36b83ad98e6fa4200c4f5b3e8b9a976f14c3198c7d7039.pdf ; Toyota T-SB-0134-08 p.1 applicability and p.2 revised quantities/marked dipstick.

- Applicable model scope: **2003–2005 Corolla** 1ZZ, with **VDS BR30E, BR32E, BR38E** as documented by Toyota. This covers model and engine family, but still requires private exact vehicle VDS check. Canadian assembly does not itself exclude a U.S.-market car with a listed VDS.
- Engine oil drain/refill **with filter**: **4.2 L / 4.4 US qt**.
- Engine oil drain/refill **without filter**: **4.0 L / 4.2 US qt**.
- The bulletin states these specifications increased by **0.5 L / approximately 0.5 US qt**.
- Old oil-level gauge **15301-22030**; revised oil-level gauge **15301-22050**. The updated part has a specific identification mark indicated in the bulletin p.2 diagram.
- This replaces the earlier research statement that the revised capacity was unknown. **Do not add a fixed 0.5 qt to an already-filled engine without inspecting the gauge and confirming the applicable bulletin.** Refill quantity is not dry capacity and must be checked against a correctly matched dipstick and appropriate service procedure.

## PCV valve follow-up

Toyota-part supplier catalog confirms:
- **12204-22041** marked 22041, supersedes **12204-22040**.
- **12204-22051** marked 22051, supersedes **12204-22050**.
- Both appear across broad Corolla 2004 model fit lists. They are **not proven interchangeable**; read actual valve stamp and use Toyota VIN EPC before replacement. There is no evidence from a Toyota bulletin of superior oil-consumption or durability performance relative to healthy original.
