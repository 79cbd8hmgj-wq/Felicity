## Five new engineering classes

### UPG-226 — Automatic headlamp and wiper-triggered lighting

Intellitronix **HL10001** manufacturer page lists a universal 12V ambient light sensor with automatic headlight and parking lamp on/off, wiper-triggered switching, positive or ground side control and manual override. Maker displayed **$128.93** before installation.  
Source: https://www.intellitronix.com/product-page/automatic-headlight-switch

A healthy 2004 Corolla's existing manually activated lighting does not automatically respond to reduced sunlight. Conditional benefit: eliminate manual switching on poor-weather/twilight drives. **Critical gate:** first obtain the TMMC June-2003 wiring diagram for DRL, manual stalk, headlight and tail-lamp relays. A universal 12V module is *not* pin-compatible proof. Retain factory manual operation, safe automatic-off and battery protection, and verify lights under all legitimate DRL/headlight settings. This is not adaptive high beam and does not reduce electrical consumption.

### UPG-227 — Dedicated Class I 1¼-inch rear receiver architecture

**CURT 11265** has a first-party 2004 Corolla sedan application (2003–2019 range), uses existing frame-rail weld nuts without drilling and accepts appropriate Class I 1¼-inch rack/carrier interfaces. The hitch maker lists **2,000 lb trailer weight** and **200 lb tongue weight as ratings for the hitch**, not the vehicle.  
Manufacturer: https://www.curtmfg.com/part/11265  
Manufacturer instructions: https://assets.curtmfg.com/masterlibrary/112653/installsheet/112653_INS.pdf  
US 2004 Corolla retailer: https://www.etrailer.com/Trailer-Hitch/Toyota/Corolla/2004/C11265.html (displayed $199.84, not an all-in quote).

**Special caution:** CURT's original sheet requires **18050 stabilizing straps for non-trailer loads**, warns not to exceed the *vehicle* manufacturer's towing capacity, and provides an original 4-fastener torque of **86 ft-lb**. This is not a blanket authorization to tow 2,000 lb with a 2004 Corolla: consult the specific owner manual and use the current hitch sheet. Rear impact, corrosion, tongue-lever force and frame weld nuts require inspection before fitting. A cargo/bike rack can create more load moment than a centered tongue measurement suggests.

### UPG-228 — Separately fused auxiliary 12V electrical distribution

**Blue Sea Systems ST Blade 5026** has **12 individually ATO/ATC-fused branches**, a labeled protective cover, negative return bus, **100A aggregate** limit and **30A per branch** maximum at 32VDC. Genuine manufacturer documentation: https://www.bluesea.com/products/5026 . Example distributor 5026 $57.70 at https://www.jamestowndistributors.com/product/product-detail/11685, price excludes wiring/fuses/relay/feeder/main protective device.

A correctly planned supplemental panel could consolidate future aftermarket heat/sensors/charger wiring and improve diagnostic clarity versus scattered fuse taps. It **does not increase alternator capacity** or make an already unsafe circuit safe by itself. Independent upstream circuit protection, circuit-specific fuse values, cable size, thermal environment, splash protection and ignition isolation are mandatory. Exact TMMC routing/installation place remains unknown.

### UPG-229 — Timed heated exterior mirrors

**Frost Fighter ThermaSync 2712-HRVM** is a current 12V dual-mirror defroster controller with **20-minute automatic turnoff**, a separate override switch and product page **$90.05** price. Source: https://frostfighter.com/2712-hrvm-thermasync-control-and-switch . Universal 12V mirror heating grids are available from https://www.raneystruckparts.com/heated-side-view-mirror-heater-element-grid/ . Replacement glass catalogs for 2004 Corolla commonly specify **without heat**: https://www.partsgeek.com/catalog/2004/toyota/corolla/body_mechanical_-ar-_trim/door_mirror_glass.html .

The two-piece technology adds winter defog/defrost functionality without replacing the mirror with a blind-spot add-on. It is **not** an exact Corolla heater kit: original mirror glass, backing and moving plate dimensions and available rear clearance have not been authenticated; retained door gaiter flex and current draw/fuse are unresolved. Wrong heater grid, glass temperature or wire passage could damage the factory mirrors. Professional custom electrical fitting is appropriate. Listed $90.05 is **controller only**, not the price for two functioning heated mirrors.

### UPG-230 — Custom contoured snow/saltwater footwell containment

WeatherTech's first-party **2004 Corolla** selector lists custom molded FloorLiners. Primary: https://www.weathertech.com/shop/2004_toyota_corolla/ . Separate first-party product page explains raised floor/side coverage: https://www.weathertech.com/floorliner-digitalfit-toyota-corolla.html?dwvar_floorliner-digitalfit-toyota-corolla_color=Grey&dwvar_floorliner-digitalfit-toyota-corolla_fitmentopt1answerid=642-4709_No&dwvar_floorliner-digitalfit-toyota-corolla_settype=1stRowDriverandPassenger&year=2003 .

Distinct from UPG-205's Toyota *damaged-retainer repair*: this is an *added molded liquid-containment system* to reduce salt/slush ingress into otherwise sound factory carpeting. The correct 2004 Corolla floor-vent and retention option must be ordered; all liners need positive retention, absence of stacking and verification of complete accelerator and brake-pedal clearance. Remove and dry accumulated water; a liner alone does not address preexisting under-carpet corrosion. No experimental corrosion life or cabin NVH figure claimed.

## Classification and research consequence

- **Higher-confidence maker application:** UPG-227 has a specific first-party 2004 Corolla sedan bolt-on part with installation guide; UPG-230 has a 2004 Corolla manufacturer floorliner selector. These are model-year references, **not** June-2003 subject-car installation evidence.
