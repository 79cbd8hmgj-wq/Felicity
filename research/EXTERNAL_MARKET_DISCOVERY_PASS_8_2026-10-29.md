## Source-backed new physical functions

### UPG-252 — improved stock-housing light distribution using street-legal 9006 halogen, not LED

New information from **outside project:** [AutoZone's 2004 Corolla headlamp list](https://www.autozone.com/collision-body-parts-and-hardware/headlight/toyota/corolla/2004) and [Advance's 2004 Corolla headlamp selection](https://shop.advanceautoparts.com/find/2004-toyota-corolla-headlights) both show **9006/HB4** halogen low-beam accessories. Later-build original Toyota 2004 manual has **9006 at 55 W**. AutoZone current price **Sylvania XtraVision 9006XV-2 $43.99 / 2 bulbs**, or alternative **SilverStar Ultra 9006SU-2 $61.99**.

[Sylvania manufacturer FAQ](https://www.sylvania-automotive.com/product-faq.html) explains its high-performance halogens operate within regulated bulb specifications and that, compared with basic versions, higher performance **trades shorter operating life**; headlamp reflector condition and aiming materially determine visibility. Specific maker claim of better down-road beam pattern and contrast is **not a healthy 2004 Corolla measured beam-intensity or stopping distance test**. Same halogen bulb still uses nominal **55W per low beam**: this does **not** serve the owner's separate low-power-LED goal, and is distinct from `UPG-123` lower wattage and `UPG-124` legally compliant LED study. An existing clean, properly aimed OEM bulb may already deliver adequate output; measure/compare before purchase. Never install higher-wattage off-road 9006 bulbs or cut/rewire original DRL housing.

**Verdict:** plausible simple parts-store experiment, **fitment=candidate**, `purchase_ready=no` until original lamp check. Not an efficiency gain; shortened life is a material penalty.

### UPG-253 — actual harness damage *deterrence*, not improved ground wire

[Honda factory service bulletin reproduced](https://lemon-manuals.la/Honda/2001/Civic%20DX%2C%202D%20Coupe%2C%20Standard/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis%20%28Single%20Page%29/Quick%20Lookups/Technical%20Bulletins/Technical%20Service%20Bulletins/Other%20Variant/System/Rodent%20Deterrent%20Tape%20For%20Wire%20Harnesses%20%28A10080B%29/) describes treated **capsaicin tape `4019-2317`**, with successive turns overlapped **half tape width**, applied to affected harness **after original rodent damage is repaired**. [Honda genuine dealer catalog](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/genuine/honda~tape~rodent~19mm~20m~4019-2317.html) quotes **$40.72** 19mm x20m roll; another vendor reports discontinued, **availability unverified**.

[Advance Auto Parts lists 16oz Infinity Shields `ISP16PAA`](https://shop.advanceautoparts.com/p/infinity-shields-protects-rodent-deterrent-surface-spray-16-oz.-peppermint-scent-isp16paa/50340836-P?brandName=Infinity+Shields&brandUrlName=infinity-shields), a distinct **peppermint scent surface deterrent** intended for engine wiring, rubber, cables; claimed up to **30 days**, but no local price/pickup or independent same-car prevention rate. This is not a Honda OEM wiring repair solution nor proof aromatic spray permanently protects engine compartment. Do not conflate the two materials: both address rodent avoidance, so **one UPG function**, not separate marketing IDs.

**Material risks:** chemical hazards to pets/occupants/workers, skin irritation and harness material/temperature compatibility, wrapping already damaged exposed wires without repairing them, spray contamination of MAF connectors/belts/air intake/hot exhaust. Vehicle current rodent evidence uninspected. Store as conditionally relevant inexpensive DIY prevention only where infestation/exposure is real.

### UPG-254 — supplemental magnetic ATF post-cooler filter

[Magnefine manufacturer](https://magnefinefilters.com/) documents a low-pressure ATF cooler return line filter with both a permanent magnet and fiber-media particulate separation and internal bypass. [Magnefine USA retail listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/5444571999) gives universal **3/8in `3810000` $25.95** hardware. New function beyond UPG-071 A245E existing **inside-pan strainer**: post-cooler return filtration of fine particles, especially potentially useful after a **transmission or cooler contamination event**. This is not a different branding of the steering return filter (UPG-231), which operates in a different hydraulic circuit.

**Critical risk:** Adds two physical cuts/clamped joints into a healthy hot ATF line, changing pressure loss and risking transmission starvation/severe leak. No source demonstrates the **exact 2004 June TMMC A245E** uses a correctly sized 3/8in serviceable rubber *return* segment or can accept added resistance at worst-case cold viscosity, no long-term life benefit measured. If it should be installed after an actual contaminated transmission rebuild, require Toyota OEM hydraulic cooler-line diagram, return flow and pressure manufacturer restrictions, correct ATF-resistant rated line/clamps and installed cold/hot pressure measurements. **No routine healthy-transmission modification recommended now.** Mark out of current DIY scope, product-only $25.95, no complete job.

### UPG-255 — sacrificial clear headlamp optic-film preservation

[WeatherTech official *2004 Toyota Corolla* LampGard](https://www.weathertech.ca/en-ca/lampgard-toyota-corolla.html?year=2004) identifies genuine vehicle-specific **`LG0870`**, described as optically clear polyurethane lens UV/scratch barrier, but the current page says **COMING SOON/unavailable**. Listed **CA $86.95** is *Canadian listed price*, not U.S. dollars or a current in-stock offer. [Rshield 2003–04 Corolla](https://www.rvinyl.com/products/toyota-corolla-2003-2004-headlight-protection-kits) describes a 12mil *clear* protective option and separately 15mil smoke/black, but current visible purchasing selectors **do not conclusively show a live clear option**; $79.99 product family price not confirmed clear kit.

**Critical wrong part correction:** AutoZone lists [WeatherTech `LG0901` for $73.99](https://www.autozone.com/p/weathertech-masking-film-lg0901/1630276), but WeatherTech [identifies `LG0901` as an early **Toyota Prius** kit](https://www.weathertech.com/lampgard-toyota-prius.html?year=2001), not Corolla. **Do not buy LG0901 for 2004 Corolla.** Only an *actual clear optical light-safe* film should be assessed; dark/smoked tinted lenses can diminish light and be illegal for road use. Headlamp optics and existing yellowing, current lens shape, beam transmission, anti-UV effectiveness and air bubbles all require independent inspection. This adds preservation of *lamp optical hardcoat*, not merely paint protection (UPG-147); however **no proof of improved healthy-stock lifetime** and correct clear item unavailable.

**Verdict:** credible separate materials function, sourcing hold / `purchase_ready=no`.

## External useful portable equipment: not counted as a vehicle modification

**AutoZone [Slime `40050`](https://www.autozone.com/p/slime-tire-pump-40050/491905), price **$34.99**.** [Slime maker](https://slime.com/products/12v-tire-inflator) describes a portable **12V compressor**, analog gauge, LED task light and ~8min refill for representative sedan tire. [O'Reilly technical catalog](https://www.oreillyauto.com/detail/c/slime/tire---wheel/tire-inflators---sealants/portable-tire-inflator---air-pump/c4c61ba85c03/slime-12-volt-tire-inflator-with-analog-gauge/sli0/40050/v/a/5629/automotive-car-2002-lincoln-ls) specifies **15A input**; original late 2004 Toyota owner manual shows **CIG front socket 15A** and rear P/POINT 15A when equipped. This is close to maximum rated outlet; **exact June 2003 fuse and socket condition unverified**. 40050 is not a puncture sealer, does not fix broken belts/sidewalls or a fully unseated tire bead. Check actual automotive outlet fuse, avoid simultaneous loads, recharge battery after use and maintain pressure to door placard.

**Classification:** This is a sensible roadside preparedness/tool purchase, **not a new performance improvement or UPG count**. The project already contains UPG-154 tire pressure monitoring and UPG-165 maintenance charging; standalone tire pressure inflation on road differs from both, but isn't permanently installed. Do not expand the conceptual engineering catalog just for a toolbox item.
