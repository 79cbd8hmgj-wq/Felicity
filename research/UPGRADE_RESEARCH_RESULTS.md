## Revisions and interchange findings

- Intake gasket 17171-22060 exists in Toyota's catalog. EG045-07's alleged 22030/0D020 replacement chain remains a **lead**: full bulletin access was blocked, and a supplier still lists 0D020. No silent supersession resolution.
- QRP distinguishes Corolla/Matrix/Vibe FWD PCV 12204-22041 from Celica/MR2 12204-88600. The 12262-22040 PCV hose is cross-listed, while the 2003–04 breather 12261-22050 is separate from later 12261-0D060.
- Engine gasket/seal listings support specific shared references, not blanket 1ZZ/2ZZ interchange. The proposed camshaft seal concept needs architecture clarification.
- TransGo SK A246 explicitly includes A245 with EPC. A245-HD2 is for older non-EPC units. Sonnax 97741-20K lists 2005-later casting 9132 and is not established for this car.
- MWR manual-transmission mount/boot or C-series differential information does not establish A245E fitment.
- Bilstein 46-182265 has a US Corolla listing but regional E12/certificate conditions need reconciliation. Whiteline BTR77's ZZE122/123 title and broad US seller coverage remain unresolved.
- Eibach's alignment-bolt page labels rear adjustment; actual strut-bolt installation location requires manufacturer instruction confirmation.
- Foreign-market LED approvals, matching bulb sockets and generic DOT labels are not evidence of an exact compliant US headlamp/source combination.
