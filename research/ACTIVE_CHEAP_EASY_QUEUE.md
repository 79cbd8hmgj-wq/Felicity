## Avoid falsely upgrading maintenance or expensive engineering

- An ordinary cabin air filter, ignition coil or oil filter changed because old one is dirty/worn is restoration. New synthetic 5W-30 vs correctly maintained modern factory-spec synthetic is not proven MPG improvement.
- Tire inflation to factory pressure is essential but restores proper operation, not a better-than-healthy-OEM modification. Do not overinflate for economy.
- A245E T-IV fill or lockup diagnosis is baseline/service, not a cheap performance upgrade. Existing technical file [A245E_DRIVETRAIN_EFFICIENCY_PASS.md](A245E_DRIVETRAIN_EFFICIENCY_PASS.md) stays as background, not immediate shopping.
- Complex hydraulic/electric steering and coolant-pump conversions belong in long-term research only.
- No legally/optically unverified headlamp/turn lamp LED conversion is automatically buy-approved. Owner requested focus elsewhere.
