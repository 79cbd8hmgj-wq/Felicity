## Value and purchasing rules

- **Highest-priority practical:** fluid film only after safely inspecting the chassis and avoiding rubber/friction parts, Rain-X exterior water repellent, optional properly selected convex mirror, and carbon cabin filter if a worthwhile odor/dust need exists. All are legitimate *small* comfort/protection changes, not measurable horsepower or major ride gains.
- **Conditional:** upgraded wipers (when existing worn/standard), NOCO GENIUS1 (if car sits), CRC protector (if corrosion exposure) and appropriate tire gauge (measurement not modification).
- **Not available as simple off-the-shelf upgrades:** full Toyota rear HVAC duct retrofit UPG-246; Canadian washer fluid-level sensor UPG-247; all 15in winter wheels/tires UPG-221 require matched wheel-and-tire package purchase/fit; CURT specific receiver/Redline hood gas springs may be **special order** through parts chains, not verified as on-shelf. Severe reported front suspension bangs require inspection, not a guess-and-swap aftermarket kit.
- **Do not assume local inventory:** AutoZone/Advance product URLs display catalog/price, not proof one is on the shelf. Pickup selection by store and 2004 Corolla fitment check required; no purchase authorization.
- **No new part is `purchase_ready=yes` solely by product SKU presence.** These leads reuse their original project UPG identifiers, not artificially created new concepts.
