- Used 2003–08 Corolla **adjustable intermittent** switch advertised at **$45**, excluding any applicable charges: https://www.ebay.com/itm/800244360098
- **Incremental benefit vs healthy factory fixed-delay switch:** selectable wipe interval improves comfort and visibility control under varying light rain. It does NOT imply superior engine performance, fuel MPG, or longer motor lifespan.
- **Critical fitment:** owner's 2004 Corolla LE was June 2003 Canada-built (NAP); Toyota catalog contains model and production-specific variants. The eBay listing's title does **not** authenticate exact part number, connector pinout, intermittent relay logic or stalk function for this car.
- **DIY gate:** steering-column trim removal and electrical connector replacement may be basic/intermediate, but installation time cannot honestly be declared <30 min until service steps and compatibility proven. Do not tamper with SRS / clock spring wiring or remove steering wheel based on unverified instructions.
- **Verdict:** research-only, potentially useful inexpensive OEM function upgrade; verify original 84652 number and donor exact part, wire connector and used item condition before buying.
