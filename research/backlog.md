# Research Queue

## P0 — identify the car
- [ ] Resolve 2002 versus 2004 model year discrepancy using VIN / certification label.
- [ ] Determine trim, production month, installed engine and transmission.
- [ ] Establish current mileage and service records.

## P1 — safety and condition
- [ ] Inspect structural corrosion, brake lines, pads/shoes, tires, steering/suspension, lighting, warning lamps.
- [ ] Measure present oil level and investigate any persistent oil consumption or leaks before driving with low oil.
- [ ] Perform VIN-specific open safety recall lookup at https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls (older completed recalls may not appear).

## P2 — repair readiness
- [ ] Locate the correct year/market Toyota factory repair manual and electrical wiring diagrams.
- [ ] Verify fluid specs, capacities and torque procedures against that vehicle's exact configuration.
- [ ] Build a component parts catalog with OEM part numbers, supersessions and verified interchange.
- [ ] Build diagnostic flowcharts for common failures, separating observed faults from reputation/forum anecdotes.

## P3 — economical long-term reliability
- [ ] Document preventive maintenance by mileage/time and severe-service use.
- [ ] Research battery capacity/fitment and charging-system test procedure.
- [ ] Research street-legal, optically compatible low-wattage lighting options; check DRL/PWM behavior before LED conversion.
- [ ] Track cost, tool requirements, difficulty, risk and sourcing for each planned repair.

## Research record schema
Question | Exact vehicle applicability | Claim | Primary source | Secondary corroboration | Confidence | Last checked | Next verification step
