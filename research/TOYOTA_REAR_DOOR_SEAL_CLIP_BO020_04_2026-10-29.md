# Toyota BO020-04 — rear door opening weatherstrip retention countermeasure

**Research date:** 2026-10-29  
**Candidate:** UPG-213 / FCM-023  
**Target:** June 2003 TMMC-built U.S. 2004 Corolla LE, model ZZE130L-DEPNKA. Actual rear opening seals uninspected. **Research status: D / incomplete parts and exact production threshold; not purchase-ready.**

## What Toyota documented

**Toyota USA BO020-04**, dated **Nov 30, 2004**, title **REAR DOOR WEATHERSTRIP LOOSE**, applies in its narrative to **2003–2005 Corolla (TMMC)** before the bulletin's production change VIN. A corresponding **Toyota Canada 2149R**, issued Dec 3, 2004 and revised in Nov 2005, describes the same issue and TMMC model-year group. These are two markets' documents for the same underlying condition; not two separate upgrades.

- Problem: rear **door opening weatherstrip** (body-aperture rubber seal, NOT the moving side-window glass run) can come loose near the rear **C-pillar / quarter-panel flange**.
- Toyota's remedial procedure: gently peel back the rear opening weatherstrip near the C-pillar; **add two specifically called-out retaining clips** on the quarter panel flange; reseat the original door weatherstrip over the new retainers. It does not prescribe wholesale door-seal replacement or global spray adhesive. Applicability is symptom-based.
- Engineering mechanism: added mechanical retention where the factory seal could release, plausibly improving local sealing and durability of seal seating. **No measured sound reduction, leak reduction, cabin comfort, or adhesive comparison**.
- The US and Canadian bulletin tables exist as **images** in the accessible archive. The accessible text establishes model years, assembly plant and procedure, but **not the genuine clip part number, exact production VIN, count per door vs procedure location, or current dealer availability**. Do **not** misidentify unrelated `67867-12150` *door-glass/weatherstrip retainers* as the bulletin's body-opening clips: location/function differs. The bulletin says install 2 clips in the illustrated location; whether that is total or per opening is not independently resolved without diagrams.
- Although the car was built June 2003, and both document narrative and factory plant match, **the bulletin's hidden VIN threshold still has to be verified** before a fitment assertion.

## Decision

A **real small and potentially useful factory-engineered quality fix** for the exact plant and model-year family, which is distinct from previously researched **NV006-05 wind-noise door-glass run improvement** and UPG-209 sill paint-protection film. **Do not install absent a loose seal; do not substitute generic window-run clips.**
