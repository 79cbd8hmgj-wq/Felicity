## Complete DIY feasibility and support cost

| Job type | Tools and supporting work that prevent an item price becoming a complete-job claim |
| --- | --- |
| Brake bleeders, fluid, rotors or grease | Safe jack/stands, wheel/brake tools, correct torque, matching hardware, sufficient correct fluid and bleed equipment; rotor axle quantity and pad pairing |
| Heat-shrink hose clamp | Measure OD over the fitting; hose-end access, heat gun, fluid handling and refill/pressure testing where opened; compare with existing spring clamp |
| Line sleeve, insulation or small slip-film patch | Correct diameter/profile, temperature/adhesive compatibility, secure retention and clearance; opening a fluid line makes a continuous sleeve more involved |
| Auxiliary thermal system/filter | Flow direction, hose/port ratings, clamps, rigid bracket, fluids/bleeding, routing and temperature/pressure evidence; thermostat alone is not a kit |
| Lumbar / rain / window controls | Actual seat or early wiring diagram, trim tools, protected fused feeds and test procedure; rain/window automation remains advanced |
| Wheels and tires | Load/lug/hub fit, compatible tires and professional mount/balance; do not report one tire or bare wheel as a whole-car solution |
| 120V preheat | Correct pan surface and oil-volume limits, secure heat-sink contact, approved cable/GFCI supply, timer if allowed and unplug/removal routine |
