# Cheap/easy fuel and system efficiency — non-LED evidence pass (2026-10-25)

**Owner direction:** move past LED conversion. **Scope:** 2004 US Corolla LE, 1ZZ-FE, A245E, Canadian assembly June 2003. Easy (<30min, simple tools) and inexpensive (<$50 per modification) preferred. Benefit measured or assessed versus **healthy factory vehicle**, while owner's photographed **215/45R18** aftermarket tire and cone-style intake require a second current-configuration comparator. No rust/vehicle-condition assessment.

## Decision and testing protocol

- **Recommendation-ready in cheap/easy non-LED category:** reflective parked-car windshield shade, **for thermal comfort and reduced solar heat soak**, not unproved quantified MPG.
- **Replacement choice, not hardware upgrade:** appropriately API/ILSAC-licensed correct 5W-30 at the next service.
- **Test/analysis instead of buying gadgets:** verify owner-tire nominal size and odometer scaling; log tank-to-tank **gallons/100 miles** with GPS-calibrated distance, comparable speeds/temperature/weather/fuel. Track hot-soak and cooldown time with vs without shade on similar sunny days if quantifying A/C reduction.
- **Not under-$50:** true low-rolling-resistance tire package and lightweight correctly-fitting wheels; maintain in longer-term project.
- **Not demonstrated:** MPG gains from oil additives, premium ignition, high-flow intake and replacement sensors against healthy OE.

**Fitment evidence vs benefit evidence:** a sunshade is a physical add-on with testable heat-load benefit in healthy parked cars; matching the manufacturer's application does not prove MPG savings. The tire size discrepancy is geometry, not a free upgrade recommendation.
