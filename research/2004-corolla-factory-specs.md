### U.S.-market brochure cross-check
The 2004 U.S. Corolla brochure identifies CE/S/LE, 1.8L VVT-i four-cylinder (130 hp @ 6,000 rpm, 125 lb-ft @ 4,200 rpm), FWD, 5-speed manual standard and 4-speed automatic available. Source: https://www.auto-brochures.com/makes/Toyota/Corolla/Toyota_US%20Corolla_2004.pdf

### Important boundaries
- This is **not** the 2002 Corolla specification sheet. Fluids, headlights, engine hardware, and other values can vary across model years/markets.
- Model-specific service procedures, fastener torques other than wheel nuts, part numbers, and maintenance intervals need separate primary-source verification.
- Oil and coolant recommendations here reflect the originally published manual, not a blanket recommendation for every later product.
- Never use a generic 2004 figure over the vehicle's installed equipment / certification label.
