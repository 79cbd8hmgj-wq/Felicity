# Healthy-OEM upgrade shortlist — preliminary

**Date:** 2026-10-25. Vehicle 2004 Corolla LE, June 2003, 1ZZ-FE/A245E. This list applies the owner's new value criterion: incremental gain over a *healthy* OEM setup, never a worn or defective baseline.

| Upgrade | Expected incremental effect versus healthy OEM | Install requirements | Value verdict / evidence gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Progress 62.2150 24mm rear bar | Greater rear roll stiffness; sharper turn-in/rotation possible; actual cornering improvement unmeasured | Raise/support rear; bar and rear axle mount access; torque manufacturer bolts; check handling/clearance | **Promising** for handling goals; may increase oversteer; no controlled test |
| Eibach 8271.140 four springs | Lower nominal ride height/center of gravity and changed roll/response; reduced travel and possible ride harshness | All four spring/strut units; safe spring compression; torque/clearance checks; alignment | **Conditional** if owner wants lowered handling; no quantified healthy-OEM comparison |
| KYB Excel-G replacement dampers | Healthy-stock-equivalent service parts; no proven better damping than healthy OE | Strut removal/spring handling, alignment, correct four-corner parts | **Not proven an upgrade**, likely standard replacement |
| FRAM XG4967 | Maker filtration/media specs; no verified reduced engine wear versus quality OE filter | Normal oil/filter procedure | **Premium unjustified without fair comparative data** |
| Toyota 17171-22060 revised manifold gasket | Factory documented leak-related design revision, not horsepower; no benefit if healthy original gasket remains leak-free | Intake manifold removal, clean surfaces, torque/sequence, leak verification | **Conditional**, appropriate when replacing a related seal; June EPC proof remains |
| TRD supercharger historical system | Real potential power/torque above healthy 1ZZ naturally aspirated; gains not measured on specific vehicle | Complete compatible blower/drive/fueling, exact TRD ECU, installation/tuning, emissions/legal review | **High upside, research only**; kit completeness/current viability unknown |
| TransGo SK A246 | Targets valve-body hydraulics; no substantiated benefit for properly shifting healthy A245E | Advanced valve-body hydraulic procedure; verify EPC first | **Not justified as universal upgrade** |

Fitment status in upgrade_research.csv does not establish measured performance. Avoid ranking parts as worth buying based only on a product listing or manufacturer claims. Future entries should include healthy-OEM baseline, incremental result, install/tool burden, incremental spend, and evidence quality.
