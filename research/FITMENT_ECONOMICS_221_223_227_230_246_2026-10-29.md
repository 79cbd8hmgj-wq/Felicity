## 2. Redline Corolla hood struts UPG-223 — exact manufacturer procedure and tools

**Maker direct product:** Redline `21-27001-02` **QuickLIFT PLUS**, $89.95, 2003–08 Corolla. Official **May 2024 Rev1.5** manufacturer installation PDF:
https://www.redlinetuning.com/v/vspfiles/templates/Redline%20New/images/21-27001.pdf

Kit is **not** a no-drill gas-strut snap-on. It requires two gas springs, **four supplied 3/16in multi-grip rivets**, two hood brackets, matched ball-studs/nuts. Maker explicitly prescribes the hood frame bracket drilling (`#11` or nominal `3/16in` bit) and enlarging each **inner-fender** ball-stud mounting hole to **5/16in**, followed by corrosion-protecting the fresh holes. Drill depth matters to avoid denting exterior hood panel. Strut orientation and retaining clip need checking. Model-specific instruction is stronger than an undifferentiated kit listing.

Maker also offers recommended hand riveter **`38-00047` at $14.95** if one is not owned; full **kit + optional riveter $104.90**, but paint, drill/bits, taxes and work time separate. Installation skill intermediate, not eligible for subject purchase approval without confirming hood/inner-fender rust and structure.

## 3. CURT rear Class I 11265 — structural mounting, not just 2,000 lb sticker

CURT first-party application and original **2013 illustrated instructions** apply to **2004 Corolla sedan**:
- https://www.curtmfg.com/part/11265
- https://assets.curtmfg.com/masterlibrary/112653/installsheet/112653_INS.pdf

Factory-provided mounting design requires **four `M12-1.25×30 mm` bolts with conical toothed washers** into existing **rear frame-rail weld nuts**. No new drilling; remove tow loops if present, clean threads, maker **86 ft-lb** torque for listed supplied hardware (confirm latest supplied installation guide). The illustrated manufacturer's time estimates **10min professional / 20min novice** assume sound, accessible bolts and do **not** account for 23 years of New York salt or repairs.

The **2,000lb towing / 200lb tongue** figures are the **hitch's** maximum rated capacities, *not* Toyota's rated Corolla capacity. The owner's available Toyota OM12859U (October-2003-on) lists **1,500lb towing** and **850lb total vehicle load**, but its production scope is not June 2003. The older CURT manual repeatedly says the vehicle's own applicable limit governs. All non-trailer wheel-less loads require suitable **CURT 18050 stabilizing straps** according to the original sheet; bike racks also impose lever moments and rack/hitch attachment compatibility. The $199.84 retailer hitch listing is **only the receiver**, not strap/rack/inspection.

**Decision:** Strong model-year external fit, but **strict safety go/no-go** for frame-rail corrosion, crack or bad captive nuts and intended rack weight. Retain candidate purchase readiness `no`. No assumption it is safe to tow 2,000 lb.
