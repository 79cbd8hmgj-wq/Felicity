## New findings of importance

- **Avoid false simplicity in installation.** MagnaFlow 15807 instructions expressly call for cutting the stock exhaust, contrary to any simple 'unbolt and replace' summary.
- **Identify full-vs-front hose kits.** Goodridge 21089 seller includes front and rear and rear-drum qualifier. StopTech 950.44005 seller identifies a front hose set. They cannot be assumed to supply identical replacements.
- **Fitment evidence is not benefit evidence.** Akebono OEM-equivalent friction media and Ultra Racing brace fitment are real product leads but do not prove substantive performance gains.
- **Verified benefit hierarchy:** a manufacturer-approved 24mm bar actually changes stiffness; a brake hose has a plausible compliance benefit; an exhaust manufacturer's dyno claim is **not** a quantified before/after number without original test details.
