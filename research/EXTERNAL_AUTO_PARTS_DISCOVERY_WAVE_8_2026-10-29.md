## UPG-252 — Sylvania XtraVision compliant low-beam comparison

AutoZone `2004 Toyota Corolla` lists XtraVision **9006XV-2** with a 2-pack price **$43.99**:
https://www.autozone.com/collision-body-parts-and-hardware/headlight/toyota/corolla/2004

Sylvania manufacturer has **9006 XtraVision** original-type 12-month-warranty halogen bulb, and **claims** more downroad visibility versus Sylvania Basic **without compromising bulb life** (the manufacturer's stronger SilverStar tiers may use different color/coating and service-life trade-offs):
https://www.sylvania-automotive.com/headlights/

**Clarification/correction vs initial draft:** Do not broadly assert XtraVision has a *shorter life* because it is brighter. The maker specifically says XtraVision does **not compromise bulb life** relative to Basic; no independent lifetime test on the Corolla is available. SilverStar Ultra has a different color design and $61.99 at AutoZone. Also not a low-watt upgrade: standard Toyota later-production headlamp charts and retailer inventory identify **9006/HB4 55W** low beams. Verify original **June 2003** headlamp/reflector (the supplied `OM12859U` is explicitly **October 2003 onward**), inspect lens clarity, clean ground, check charging voltage and aim correctly before assuming incremental range. Pair replacement should use safe access/no fingerprints on glass. Compliant source type only; do not substitute 9006 LED capsules of uncertain reflector photometry or invent candela/lux/distance numbers.

**Full assessment:** Useful at the next bulb replacement; cheap/store-accessible and preserves OE circuitry. Insufficient case for throwing out perfectly healthy quality new 9006 bulbs solely for unmeasured claims.

## UPG-253 — Honda OEM rodent-resistant tape / Advance underhood spray

Honda service information **A10080B** (August 2010) identifies **4019-2317**, a capsaicin-treated harness tape applied with **half-overlap** after repair of a rodent-damaged harness. This is **cross-manufacturer repair engineering**, not evidence Toyota authorized the exact 2004 1ZZ wiring or that capsaicin guarantees future animal avoidance. Source transcription:
https://lemon.dogeware.me/Honda/2006/S2000/Repair%20and%20Diagnosis%20%28Single%20Page%29/Quick%20Lookups/Technical%20Bulletins/Technical%20Service%20Bulletins/Engine%20Control%20Systems/Rodent%20Deterrent%20Tape%20For%20Wire%20Harnesses%20%28A10080B%29/

Dealer lists the Honda special tape **4019-2317**, nominal 19mm×20m, price **$40.72**, though other listings say discontinued and physical stock unverified:
https://www.hondapartsnow.com/genuine/honda~tape~rodent~19mm~20m~4019-2317.html

Advance also lists **Infinity Shields `ISP16PAA`** peppermint-scent rodent surface spray as a generic vehicle/engine bay protective treatment lasting **up to** 30 days **per seller claim**:
https://shop.advanceautoparts.com/p/infinity-shields-invisible-shield-prevents-rodent-chewing-nesting-and-engine-bay-damage-isp16paa/50340836-P

**Engineering gates:** Verify chew/nesting evidence first. Do not apply spicy tape to hot exhaust, rotating belts, exposed connectors, O2 sensor, SRS/airbag loom or areas where trapping moisture damages wiring. Use gloves/eye protection and appropriate material compatibility SDS. Never coat electrical connector mating contacts with unknown repellent. Priority conditional on an actual local rodent hazard; garage storage practices and inspecting for damage remain important. No percentage of prevented chew events is established.

## UPG-254 — Magnefine ATF return external filter (NOT approved)

Maker https://magnefinefilters.com/ and supplier technical discussions identify an externally mounted magnet and filtration media with bypass, offered in multiple hose diameters; **3810000** is nominal 3/8in. External support for standard sizes and universal installation:
https://ifjf.net/products/3-8-magnetic-inline-automatic-transmission-power-steering-filter-for-universal-light-truck-and-car-3810000-58953-tf101m
https://www.motortrend.com/how-to/129-1002-automatic-transmission-and-power-steering-filters

**Engineering difference from UPG-071:** independent return-flow filter is downstream of internal original A245E strainer, useful in theory when cooler holds debris after a failed/remanufactured transaxle. Unlike a simple drain/fill, it **requires cutting the ATF return** and introducing two hose-to-barb junctions, directional flow, external mount and future replacements. The named universal 3/8in device is **not fitment proof**; official Toyota A245E external line ID, regulator, actual operating hydraulic flow/cold viscosity, permissible backpressure and safe cooler-flow sequence are unmeasured. Hot pressurized T-IV fluid leaks can destroy an otherwise healthy transmission, and a new inline filter may add failure modes.

**Decision:** `outside_current_diy_scope`, **purchase_ready=no**. Reserve for actual transaxle contamination or a validated post-rebuild cooler-cleaning plan supervised by an A245E specialist, only after OEM/manufacturer compatibility and flow checks. No claim it lengthens this healthy A245E life or shifts better.

## UPG-255 — Corolla-specific *clear* headlamp protection film

WeatherTech 2004 Toyota Corolla LampGard identifies **`LG0870`**, **coming soon / unavailable** at its 2004 maker page, displaying **CA$86.95**:
https://www.weathertech.ca/en-ca/lampgard-toyota-corolla.html?year=2004

**Critical negative interchange evidence:** AutoZone product code `LG0901` looks plausible in its retailer listing, but WeatherTech maker identifies **`LG0901` with the 2001/2002 Prius**, *not the Corolla*. Do not purchase LG0901 for the Corolla. Official manufacturer:
https://www.weathertech.com/lampgard-toyota-prius.html?year=2001

Rshield maker has a specific **Toyota Corolla 2003–2004** precut headlight film family, but the actual selectable product options appear to show smoked/darker variants and clear 12mil text description separately; **do not mark transparent film currently orderable or legally confirmed** without supplier SKU:
https://www.rvinyl.com/products/toyota-corolla-2003-2004-headlight-protection-kits

Film might protect a sound, non-oxidized lens from future UV pitting and grit. That is a *hypothesis* against a healthy OE hardcoated polycarbonate surface; it cannot undo clouding, and any scatter/bubbles/yellowing or smoke film could be worse at night. Only fully transparent optically suitable road-legal film should be considered after lens profile and manufacturer availability are authenticated. No current purchase recommendation.

## Additional external find: 12V tire inflator, no new UPG

**Slime 40050** AutoZone **$34.99**, universal compact 12V tire pump, LED lamp, published 8min typical car tire to 35psi:
https://www.autozone.com/p/slime-tire-pump-40050/491905

Its manufacturer service instructions require **minimum 15A 12V outlet**; a separate reseller lists nominal 15A current:
https://images.oreillyauto.com/parts/img/documents/sli/40050_instructions_new.pdf
https://www.oreillyauto.com/detail/c/slime/tire---wheel/tire-inflators---sealants/portable-tire-inflator---air-pump/c4c61ba85c03/slime-12-volt-tire-inflator-with-analog-gauge/sli0/40050/v/a/5329/automotive-truck-1989-ford-f-250

The Corolla's **October 2003+** manual shows a front `CIG` **15A** fuse; that is **not the exact early June 2003 car**. This pump could draw the whole available fused rating, requiring checking actual fuse/outlet and not using other loads concurrently. Manufacturer instructions say shut off and cool after 10 minutes. It does **not repair punctures or cord damage** and should not be used to make a damaged unsafe tire drivable.

Treat as useful **roadside kit** supporting existing UPG-154/pressure-monitoring work, not novel installed vehicle improvement. Product not purchase-verified for local store.
