## Corrected earlier assumptions
1. **Factory 2004 Corolla plugs already long-life iridium.** Earlier 'premium plugs may help power' narrative has no credible healthy-OE comparative support, and Denso regional Corolla Altis IK16TT listing must not be transferred to this US car.
2. **TYC 800006P is particulate, not charcoal.** Do not classify it as the premium activated-carbon solution.
3. **Sensors:** the high-value simple modification is reading stock sensor data with an OBD-II tool, not buying aftermarket replacements for healthy sensors.
4. **LED headlights:** legal replacement source requirements cannot be bypassed by 'DOT' marking, claimed lumen value, plug geometry or wattage alone.
5. **FRAM's 99%+ test result is a group-average under stated conditions**, not independent comparison proving an XG4967 upgrade over Toyota OE in this engine.

## What can be bought based on benefits now?
- **Plug-in OBD-II scan display/data logger:** provides real data not on stock dashboard, simplest install. Specify software features and confirmed PID support before selecting product.
- **Interior/trunk LEDs:** when correct base, optics and dimming verified, lower energy use and selectable output. For road-facing signals further testing required.

Everything else needs a specific controlled comparator or the exact premium product identified (especially charcoal cabin filter and headlight optical performance). Not proven to have *no* benefit: currently **no demonstrated healthy-OE advantage**.
