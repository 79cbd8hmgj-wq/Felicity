# PR #1 Technical Review — Tranche 2: Suspension and Radio Fitment

**Review date:** 2026-10-25. Applies to June-2003-built US 2004 Corolla LE. This is manufacturer-document review, not vehicle installation verification.

## UPG-084 — Eibach 8271.140 Pro-Kit
- **Primary document:** https://suspension.eibach.com/storage/instructions/D2292.pdf, document D2292 Rev1, printed/PDF page 1.
- Explicit application: **2003–2008 Toyota Corolla CE, S & LE 4-cylinder**. This **resolves the earlier trim-specific LE listing gap** at manufacturer level (but not the exact vehicle/aftermarket-wheel clearance).
- Detailed BOM: front springs **8272.001** quantity 2; rear springs **8271.002** quantity 2; information kit EPACK; installation instructions **8271.140INST**.
- Retain original spring isolators and dampers/tubing; full alignment afterward; check wheel/tire-to-fender and brake/ABS line clearances. Document says **no front or rear bump-stop trimming**.
- Manufacturer product listing https://www.eibach.com/eps-search?make=Toyota&model=Corolla&submodel=Base&year=2003 states nominal **1.1-inch** front/rear drop and MSRP **$395**, but identifies Base selection rather than independently verifying the exact LE weight/stance outcome.
- **Evidence outcome:** 2004 LE is manufacturer-documented; **retain probable fitment** until vehicle wheel clearance, load and spring/damper setup are established. No measured Corolla LE performance benefit was authenticated.

## UPG-088 — Progress Technology 62.2150 rear anti-sway bar
- **Primary document:** https://progressauto.com/ecom/download/62.2150.pdf, Rev B dated 2022-08-09, pages 1–2.
- Explicit maker instruction coverage: **2001–2008 Toyota Corolla / Matrix**; **24 mm rear sway bar**, quantity one.
- The original 2003–08 year-header inconsistency was resolved at **manufacturer instruction level**: the maker's current instruction expressly includes 2004. Application guide https://progressauto.com/ApplicationGuideExcelComplete2-9-15-A.pdf also lists Corolla **03–08** / **62.2150 (24mm)**.
- Installation document depicts rear beam attachment with original two mounting bolts; do not extrapolate full wheel-specific clearance or steering balance from this photo.
- **Evidence outcome:** Strong model-year manufacturer support, but specific mounting condition and quantified handling effects not yet verified. Keep **probable**, not vehicle-validated.

## UPG-133 — Metra 95-8204 double-DIN dash adapter
- **Primary manufacturer product document:** https://www.metraonline.com/product/95-8204. Declared application: **2003–2008 Toyota Corolla**; standard 4-inch-tall double-DIN aftermarket receiver with ISO-mount provisions.
- Independent Metra O'Reilly fitment catalog https://metra-oreilly.com/product/95-8204 confirms Corolla 2003–08 and separates wiring options **70-1761** and **TYTO-01** depending on vehicle equipment.
- **Evidence outcome:** The physical dash kit has unusually clear model-year applicability. Matching stereo depth, factory amplifier and specific harness remain unresolved; retain **probable for full integration**, not confirmed for an arbitrary receiver.
