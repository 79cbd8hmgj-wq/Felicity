# Simple-upgrade research: filters, plugs, sensors, lamps and low-effort parts

**Date:** 2026-10-25. **Vehicle:** US 2004 Corolla LE, June 2003 build, 1ZZ-FE, A245E. This expands the researched set without treating hardware marketing as healthy-OE performance gains. Easy installation only unless stated otherwise.

**Manual caveat:** OM12859U covers October 2003-onward production, while subject vehicle built June 2003. Spark-plug/bulb entries are verified as manual references, but production applicability must be checked in early EPC/EWD. Specifically, manual bulb table printed page 244: high 9005 60W, low 9006 55W; rear stop/tail 3157, rear turn 3156, back-up 921. Printed page 253 gives SK16R11 / IFR5A11, gap 1.1mm. Factory already used long-life iridium; this substantially raises the evidence bar for claimed upgraded plugs.

## Priority research questions
1. Healthy Toyota original oil filter vs XG4967: same efficiency particle sizes, bypass flow, service-life basis, and cost — avoid transferring FRAM group-average life figures directly into engine-life estimates.
2. Spark plug part-number-specific iridium TT cross-reference versus SK16R11/IFR5A11; any *controlled Corolla comparison*, not just electrode marketing.
3. Low-watt compliant lighting with measured beam distribution, not socket-only fitment; prioritize simple interior, reverse and marker lamp functions and clarify legality.
4. Confirm exact present air filter dimensions/intake manufacturer before recommending performance replacement.
5. Sensor logging functionality by available SAE PIDs; replacement sensors are not performance modifications on healthy OEM engine.
