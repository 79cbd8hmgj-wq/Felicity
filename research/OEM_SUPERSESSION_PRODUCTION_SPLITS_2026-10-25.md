# Felicity — OEM supersession and production-split audit (2026-10-25)

**Scope:** US-market 2004 Corolla LE, Canadian June-2003 manufacture, owner-described 1ZZ-FE/A245E, Toyota ZZE130L-DEPNKA. **Status: catalog-supported candidates only, no VIN-specific fitment confirmation, physical inspection, verified durability improvement, or purchased/installed parts.** Do not treat Toyota supersession as automatic engineering improvement. Vehicle identification must still be checked against actual identification label/VIN privately.

## 1. Strongest new evidence: right-side engine mounting revision

The North American production (NAP) 2004 Corolla parts-diagram catalog distinguishes:
- **12305-0D021**, right/passenger-side engine mounting insulator, **06/2002–01/2004**, fitment `1ZZFE..ZZE130..NAP`.
- **12305-0D022**, same position, **01/2004–11/2005**, fitment `1ZZFE..ZZE130..NAP`.
- Both are listed as **replaced by 12305-0D023** in the catalog.

**Interpretation:** this is meaningful *dated design/parts-revision evidence*, directly intersecting the June-2003 vehicle. It is **NOT** evidence that 0D023 has softer rubber, better NVH or longer life than a healthy original mount. Toyota may supersede for supply, packaging, manufacturing or other reasons. Changing the right mount can affect engine position and NVH, but installation must preserve support, fastening torque and load paths. Do not order until VIN EPC, mount physical variant and any needed accompanying bracket/fastener differences are verified; replace only if actual symptoms/inspection justify it.

## 2. Automatic-specific mount split: don't order a manual mount

The same NAP catalog distinguishes:
- **Rear transmission/engine mount 12371-0D110**, 1ZZFE / ZZE130 **4FC** (4-speed automatic catalog qualifier).
- **12371-0D100**, ZZE130 **5F** manual qualifier.
- **Front mount 12361-0D100** applicable to ZZE130 NAP **4FC**; front mount **12361-0D090** references **5F**.
- **Left/transaxle mount 12372-0D110**, ZZE130 NAP **4FC**; **12372-0D100** is shown for **5F**.

**Interpretation:** a generic “fits 2004 Corolla” mount kit can have the wrong geometry/durometer/attachment fit. These are catalog leads tied to automatic equipment, not proof that later Corolla or Matrix mounts are beneficial. No swap between trim, transmission or model authorized without OEM diagram, actual bracket details and controlled noise/vibration testing.

## 3. Intake manifold: production split is not a performance retrofit

NAP manifold diagram lists:
- **17120-0D080**, intake manifold, note **(L)** and **from 05/2004** production, `1ZZFE..ZZE130..NAP`.
- **17120-22030**, note **(J)**, `1ZZFE..ZZE130..NAP`, replaced by **17120-0D040**.

The general all-2004 product catalog also lists **17120-22070 → 17120-0D080**, but combines configurations; **do not ascribe that generic supersession to this June-2003 vehicle** without checking precise production date and variant identification. Parenthetical manufacturer notes (J)/(L) must not be reinterpreted as documented performance grades. Later manifold design or weight listing alone does not establish improved airflow, economy, emissions compliance or lower induction noise on a healthy engine.

### Verification protocol

1. Obtain **private VIN-based Toyota EPC query**, production date, exact 4FC transmission qualifier and factory catalog illustration. Do not commit VIN or identifying photos.
2. Compare as-built OEM number, catalog replacement history and **required companion hardware** (gasket, bracket, pulley, O-ring, bolts, hose or connector).
3. Seek manufacturer technical service bulletins/revision notices explaining the **engineering reason**; if absent, classify as *parts availability/supersession only*, not confirmed upgrade.
4. For right mount, establish idle in D/R vibration, mount fluid/leaks/collapse, shifts/torque movement, road-noise baseline, and compare only **if replacement is required**. No at-home gear-in-drive observations with a person near moving engine or unsupported components.
5. Do not transplant later-year manifolds, automatic-to-manual mounts, Matrix/Vibe pieces or water pumps based solely on photograph, engine family, supersession listing or generic seller “fits” banner.

**Summary:** This tranche documents one important actual production split and the automatic-specific mount family, but **does not add four invented “performance upgrades.”** The database's verified-parts ledger must remain unmodified until applicability is confirmed.
