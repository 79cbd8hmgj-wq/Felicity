## Safety and prioritization

**Safety gate:** The owner has described loud bangs from both sides of the front suspension over potholes. Wheel/hub bearing and tire/steering/ball-joint/swaybar and front strut mounts, brake caliper bolts and chassis should be physically diagnosed before any handling or suspension performance changes or road tests. A cheap in-store tie-rod/bushing/strut is not a safe diagnosis.

**Highest utility for inexpensive DIY when actually needed:** Rain-X exterior glass UPG-150, convex side mirrors UPG-189, proper carbon cabin filter UPG-128, good 2004 Corolla wiper set UPG-178, weatherproof footwell liners UPG-230 (retention verified), and correctly applied corrosion protection on *sound* metal UPG-242. UPG-165 when parked long periods; UPG-136 when engine data useful.

**Appropriate parts-counter service:** PCV valve, valve gasket, oil filter, battery, serpentine belt, brake pads/rotors/fluid, battery cables and coolant parts are often stocked or orderable, but mostly **maintain/restore factory performance** and should be purchased based on condition, not to increase a concept count.

**Hard exclusions:** Heated wiper frames UPG-243 voltage limit; lithium RE-START UPG-245 poor winter economics; Monroe jounce replacement UPG-251 not a known healthy-OE ride improvement; complex ABS conversion UPG-234 and SRS repairs are outside non-professional retrofit.
