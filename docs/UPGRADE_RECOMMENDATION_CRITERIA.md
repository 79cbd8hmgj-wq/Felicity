# Upgrade recommendation criteria — benefits and installation first

Decision recorded 2026-10-25: owner prioritizes **actual benefits and installation requirements** over simple product lists. This preference is research-method guidance, not a maintenance or corrosion assessment.

For every shortlisted upgrade, report in this order:

1. **Benefits** — precisely what improves (power/torque, handling response, ride quality, durability, comfort, efficiency); distinguish measured independent results, manufacturer tests/claims, engineering inference, and restoration of worn baseline. No invented gains.
2. **Installation requirements** — tools, whether vehicle lifting is required, disassembly boundaries, special procedures, safety-dependent steps, supporting hardware, fluids, wiring/ECU calibration, and post-install verification. Clearly mark unknown torque/procedure details.
3. **DIY feasibility** — basic / intermediate / advanced / specialist, and specific skills required; never classify complex transmission or SRS work as trivial.
4. **Compatibility gates** — June 2003 production, A245E, current aftermarket intake/18-inch wheel constraints as applicable; candidate/probable/confirmed status.
5. **Downsides** — adverse effects on ride, sound, durability, braking/handling balance, emissions or road legality.
6. **Cost** — dated product and complete-install economics when sourced, separate from benefits.

Recommendation tiers:
- **Actionable conditional:** strong manufacturer application documentation, known installation method, plausible benefit, bounded remaining checks.
- **Research-only:** major fitment or installation/control/legality gaps.
- **Not supported:** no credible benefit evidence or demonstrably wrong application.

## Initial examples, not validated purchasing instructions

- **Progress 62.2150 rear 24mm bar:** chassis roll/response change expected, magnitude not quantified. Requires rear-beam access and manufacturer instructions/torque specifications; change to yaw balance may increase oversteer.
- **Eibach 8271.140 springs:** nominal manufacturer lowering and body-control change. Four corners require safe coil-spring handling, strut disassembly/assembly and alignment; 18-inch wheel clearance not yet verified.
- **KYB Excel-G struts:** damping restoration if originals are worn; full corner-by-corner SKU and upper mounts need catalog check; strut service, spring-handling and alignment.
- **FRAM XG4967 filter:** manufacturer filtration construction; routine oil-filter installation. No vehicle-specific durability improvement proven.
- **Toyota revised 17171-22060 intake gasket:** addresses documented cold-weather seal-related lean condition; intake removal and factory torque/leak procedure; exact June-build applicability pending.
- **TRD 1ZZ supercharger:** potential substantial power; specialized mechanical, fueling and matched TRD ECU installation; legacy package current legality and parts completeness unknown.
- **TransGo SK A246:** hydraulic/shift-specific valve-body kit; advanced precision transmission service, model-specific EPC confirmation and instruction verification necessary.

Research should continue independently without waiting on physical inspection. Keep condition assessments out of scope unless requested.
