# Phase 2 — engineering comparisons by outcome

See [discovery report](PHASE2_DEEP_RETAIL_DISCOVERY_2026-10-29.md) for scope and [budget catalog](PHASE2_BUDGET_DIY_CATALOG_2026-10-29.md) for price units. Numbers below are manufacturer or catalog specifications, not measured outcomes on Felicity. The CSV rows contain tool/support requirements and exact evidence-stage limitations.

## Mechanical reliability and hydraulic behavior

**Valve-body hydraulics — DSC-P2-022/023.** [TransGo SK A246](https://transgo.com/product-details/toyota-a246e-shift-kit) explicitly includes the EPC-equipped 2003–08 Corolla A245E. Its replacement valve/bushing arrangements target pressure leakage associated with wear. [A245-HD2](https://transgo.com/product-details/toyota-a245e-reprogramming-kit) is for older non-EPC A245E units and is excluded. The $90 SK kit is an advanced repair candidate: pan/valve-body work, cleanliness, correct gaskets, torque sequence and T-IV are part of the job. “Transmission removal not required” does not make it basic driveway work. No healthy-original shift-time or efficiency improvement was demonstrated.

**Thermoplastic versus spring clamps — 001/002.** [Gates 32922](https://www.gates.com/us/en/fluid-power/engine-hose/hose-clamps-and-couplings.p.7405-000000-000000.v.7405-04003.html) uses heat-shrink material with memory rather than a screw band. The small size covers roughly 21–24 mm hose OD, while 32948 is a different larger range. Select by OD over the fitting and available land, not hose nominal bore. Broad loading and thermal accommodation are plausible advantages over an overtightened worm drive; healthy Toyota spring clamps already accommodate thermal cycling. Installation needs access over the hose end and controlled heat; removal/reuse and roadside serviceability are tradeoffs. No demonstrated superiority over sound original clamps supports blanket replacement.

**Steering filtration — 063 versus original DSC-40-033.** [Carquest 20-0012FCQN](https://shop.advanceautoparts.com/p/carquest-premium-power-steering-filter-20-0012fcqn/50298779-P) describes a magnet, secondary membrane and bypass. CARDONE 20-0038F describes a serviceable metal-screen architecture; the existing research retains its 190/200-micron copy conflict. This is a meaningful architecture comparison: a finer membrane can also create more pressure drop, while a coarse cleanable screen has different capture/service properties. Obtain exact size, efficiency versus particle size, pressure drop and bypass pressure. Generic 3/8-and-1/2-inch family text does not identify this SKU's ports. Do not transfer CARDONE cleaning instructions to the Carquest element. The live Carquest item price was $53.99, with Albany pickup unavailable.

**Pump construction — 029/030.** AISIN WPT-106 ($46.97 indexed) and Duralast AWP-9173 ($78.99 indexed) are concrete replacement alternatives. Neither retrieved listing establishes a superior impeller, reduced drive power or improved seal design over a healthy original 1ZZ pump. Supplier identity and warranty may matter at replacement time; they do not establish a preventive performance benefit. Exact castings, gasket interfaces and OEM cross-references remain unresolved.

## Ride, steering precision and wheel/tire behavior

**Jounce management — 054/059.** KYB SB103 and SB102 are actual bumper/boot products available through several chains. The retrieved material establishes a foam travel-limiter architecture, not a desirable new force-deflection curve. Required comparisons are free length, entry force, compressed clearance and engagement relative to spring/damper travel, all by correct axle. Boots and a missing/damaged bumper restore protection. A new foam component is not proven to outperform a healthy factory bumper. Strut disassembly/spring handling makes this advanced work in this research classification.

**Damper technologies remain a gap.** The existing OESpectrum, Excel-G and STR.T evaluations were retained rather than recycled as new discoveries. The first two are described as restoration products; fixed STR.T damping is not adjustable or frequency-selective. Broad KONI Special Active searches did not authenticate a direct early-North-American-Corolla SKU. An adaptable damper still needs open/compressed lengths, travel, mounts, spring-seat geometry and matched damping curves. A brand-level frequency-selective technology description is insufficient evidence to fit a later-car damper. No “better ride” verdict is issued without a healthy-original comparator.

**Wheel construction — 047/048.** [Enkei J10 409-565-02](https://enkei.com/shop/wheels/performance/j10) is 15x6.5 ET38 with a dual 5x100/114.3 pattern and 72.62 mm bore. [RPF1 3796708035](https://enkei.com/shop/wheels/racing/rpf1) is 16x7 ET35, 5x100, 73 mm bore. These are product-level geometries, not confirmed Corolla installations. The manufacturer's retrieved tables give no exact weight for either. A widely repeated roughly 9.9-pound 15x7 RPF1 example has a different 4x100 pattern and cannot be assigned to the target wheel. Hub rings, correct lug seats, load rating, spoke/caliper and tire clearance, mounting and balancing add cost. Current 18-inch equipment and healthy original 15-inch equipment must be analyzed as separate baselines.

**Tire architecture — 049/050.** Continental TrueContact Tour 54 15578300000 in 195/65R15 91H has an indexed $146.99-per-tire quote; General RT45 195/65R15 91H has a $459.96 four-tire quote. The General entry uses exact retailer catalog ID 965HR5AMRT45 because the maker SKU was not verified; it must not be mixed with the 91T variant. General's wear/alignment indicators add observable information, but are not alignment correction or proof of shorter stopping distances. Current-generation construction may offer meaningful wet grip, noise and rolling resistance differences; a relevant controlled test with the same sizes/conditions remains necessary. Installation requires a tire shop, even if wheel removal is DIY.

## Braking consistency, corrosion and serviceability

**Plain, coated and metallurgy claims — 017–019/061/062.** First-party [Carquest YH145294](https://weblink.carquest.com/acl/?mfgName=BDR&partNumber=YH145294) gives about 275 mm OD, 25 mm nominal thickness, 23 mm discard, 49.3 mm height and five holes on approximately 100 mm PCD. Seller specifications for Centric 120.44114 agree on that broad geometry and identify painted-hat protection; no exact high-carbon composition was established. Raybestos 96934FZN has a manufacturer application-guide lead and seller dimensional/cross-reference evidence. Carquest YH145294C remains a coated-suffix cross-reference lead until its own drawing/application is retrieved. NAPA 880265CR remains an unverified search-context application; do not buy it as a Corolla rotor. Coating mainly improves non-swept corrosion resistance. Neither holes/slots nor the word Premium proves a braking improvement over healthy original brakes. Compare pair prices, runout limits, coating coverage and friction pairing.

**Fluid properties — 014–016.** [ATE's primary table](https://www.ate-na.com/products/brake-fluids) reports TYP 200 at 280/198 °C dry/wet and SL.6 at 265/175 °C. Their maximum -40 °C viscosities are 1400 and 700 mm²/s respectively. [Pentosin's PDS](https://crpautomotive.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Pentosin-Product-Data-Sheet-Brake-Fluid-DOT-4-LV.pdf) gives 265/170 °C and 700 mm²/s for DOT4 LV. TYP 200 emphasizes thermal reserve; SL.6/LV emphasizes cold flow. Confirm explicit suitability for the Toyota DOT3 hydraulic system and use the proper bleed procedure. No ESP-response gain is claimed for a car without ESP. The FCP SL.6 description contains a wet-Fahrenheit typo; manufacturer Celsius values control the comparison. Full fluid quantity and total service cost are unknown.

**Integral-check bleeders — 011–013.** These add a useful service function without changing brake clamping force. Russell 639570 is a pair lead at $18.99; steel Speed Bleeder SB7100 is advertised at $12 per pair; SB7100-SS is $15 each. Thus four stainless screws alone cost $60, not $15. Nominal lengths differ between steel chart, stainless store and Russell listing, so M7x1 alone is insufficient: verify seat cone, thread engagement and clearance at all four installed locations. Bleeding still requires reservoir control, correct fluid, final tightening and leak/pedal verification. Seized original screws can break. No identical healthy-car installed comparison was authenticated.

**Lubricants — 020/021.** Permatex 24112 has a silicone/ceramic formulation and the maker states rubber/plastic/metal compatibility. Sil-Glyde BK7651351 is a different consumable alternative. Toyota's actual specified locations and boot materials control use. The 99-percent noise and 3000 °F marketing claims are not Corolla results or continuous bulk-grease ratings. Sound correctly lubricated brakes are not improved simply by replacing their grease.

## Performance and efficiency

**Electrode geometry — 024–026.** A newer plug material is worth investigating, but the all-years Corolla 92274 result points to a 12 mm/26.5 mm later-engine architecture. It is not a valid 1ZZ selection. Original-type Denso SK16R11/3324 is a benchmark; Denso's IK16TT table gives 14 mm/19 mm geometry with a 1.0 mm gap, but exact 1ZZ suitability, projection and prescribed gap still need verification. No measured response/MPG/power gain beyond healthy specified iridium plugs was found.

**Accessory-drive materials — 027/028.** Dayco 5060740 and Continental 4060740 provide alternatives to the existing Gates belt lead. Confirm exact rib count, effective versus outside length, current material/profile and installed routing. Continental's branding changed from Elite to OE Technology, so an older Gatorback photo does not establish the current construction. No controlled drive-loss test supports an efficiency claim. Correct material and noise behavior can be relevant replacement criteria without becoming horsepower claims.

**Cooling and thermal bypass — 031–035.** Hayden 1009/1010 steering coolers and 679 ATF cooler have different size/packaging purposes. Extra cooling must follow measured thermal need. [Derale 13011 instructions](http://derale.com/images/stories/virtuemart/product/pdfs/13011.pdf) establish a four-port bypass arrangement, supplied 3/8 NPT-to-barb fittings and required routing/mounting precautions. Near 180 °F the bypass begins to close; this is not an instantaneous switch or proof of ideal A245E temperature. A $94.71 thermostat alone leaves brackets, hose, cooler and fluid unpriced. Hayden 3653 fan control cannot be called an efficiency upgrade when Toyota already switches the electric fan with A/C integration.

**Additives — 055–058.** Lubegard 60902, 30902, 96001 and LIQUI MOLY 20002 were examined as actual marketed chemistries rather than ignored departments. Manufacturer-hosted tests and generic friction/heat-transfer claims do not establish durable gains in a healthy 1ZZ/A245E using correct fluids. There is no purchase recommendation or calculated fuel saving. Do not dilute antifreeze for a marketing temperature result or convert ATF chemistry without relevant validation.

## Comfort and cabin climate

**Pneumatic lumbar support — 046.** Current Rostra 250-1456 is a dual-bladder architecture, distinct from older single-bladder kits and more specific than a generic replacement seat cushion. It can adjust support through the original seat foam while preserving the shell. Bladder space, restraint equipment, full seat travel, pump retention, tubing and fused wiring remain gates. An indexed installation-PDF URL returned 404, so a full verified procedure was not claimed. This is UPG-138 evidence development, not an invented extra comfort function or a medical claim.

**Cold A/C-line insulation — 008.** Four Seasons 59010 is a 30-foot asphalt-cork roll, offered at $18.41 on the retrieved AutoNation page and cataloged by Auto Value. Fisher advertises the Four Seasons line. The intervention can be performed without opening refrigerant plumbing if applied to a suitable accessible cold line. Identify the line, measure ambient pickup and keep fittings/service ports visible. No cooler-vent temperature or compressor-power saving has been measured; indiscriminate wrapping can hide leaks.

**External preheat — 051/052.** A removable magnetic 200 W Zerostart 3400017 and fixed 150 W Kats 24150 adhesive pad are different architectures. Pan material, flat contact area, oil volume, exact approved installation and electrical routing govern suitability. The pad is not automatically appropriate for a curved/ribbed pan; an unattached heater can overheat. Compare electricity use, actual cold-start thermal rise and removal/unplug routine. There is no oil-pan-to-coolant-heater equivalence or verified Corolla fuel payback.

**Active air filtration — 053.** GP5212 adds a powered recirculated filter path beyond the HVAC intake filter. The manufacturer announcement establishes the architecture, not an installed pollen-exposure result or current nearby inventory. Mounting, filter availability, noise and power load affect value. It is not protection from CO or a substitute for ventilation.

## Refinement and useful automation

**Slip film versus mass damping — 010.** [3M 5430](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/b40070582) uses low-friction UHMW film at rubbing interfaces. Retail pack 06356 is distinct from the series name. It addresses stick-slip squeaks, not airborne absorption or panel resonance. This avoids buying more mass damping to address a small rubbing joint. Trial fit, adhesive compatibility and assembly clearances matter; no change is needed at a silent joint.

**Thermal protection — 003–007/009.** Split DEI 010457 can wrap an existing connected line; continuous 010403/AeroFlow AF91-5000 may need disconnection. Hood matting, a fixed radiant sheet and an external floor shield address different heat paths. Preserve original shielding and measure exposure; radiant ratings are not continuous-contact ratings. Keep drainage and connectors visible, and avoid moisture/abrasion traps. Exact US pricing for the AeroFlow items was not authenticated; Universal's brand relationship is a sourcing lead, not stock.

**Rain/window automation — 044/045.** Legacy Hydreon RT-50 documentation specifies sensor placement and a wiper interface; Directed 530T documentation requires actual window motor circuit identification and protected wiring. A modern standalone three-wire rain sensor found in a retailer catalog is not a complete controller. Availability of a sensor does not solve park logic, switch polarity or automatic-window obstruction protection. These remain advanced architecture candidates, separate from straightforward plug-in convenience products.

**Washer delivery — 040–043/060.** Dorman 47237 has a threaded mount and wider adjustable spray than its own standard universal nozzle; it is not heated. Original-generation Toyota 85381-02100 supplies the comparison baseline, while Camry 85381-AA042 is a donor candidate rather than a Corolla supersession. Toyota 85321-26020 and Dorman 926-335 are check-valve architectures, not automatic improvements if the original system already holds prime. Measure hood aperture, nozzle angle, hose dimensions, starting delay, coverage and airflow sensitivity before changing anything.
