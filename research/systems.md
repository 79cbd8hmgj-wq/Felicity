# System Research — 2004 U.S. Corolla Automatic

This is a first-pass map, not a part-number catalog or factory workshop procedure. Applicability to the individual car must be checked against trim/build configuration and VIN privately.

## Powertrain
- **Engine reference:** Toyota 1ZZ-FE 1794 cm³, inline four, original 2004 U.S. Corolla manual p.252.
- **Valve train/ignition:** factory spark plug references DENSO SK16R11 and NGK IFR5A11, 1.1 mm gap (manual p.253). Inspection and 120k-mile plug interval documented in 1ZZ-FE service material.
- **Lubrication:** factory recommendation SAE 5W-30, original API SL Energy Conserving or ILSAC specification; drain and refill with filter 3.7 L / 3.9 US qt (manual p.253). Actual dipstick level determines safe fill, never rely solely on stated capacity.
- **Cooling:** 6.5 L total, Toyota Genuine Long Life Coolant or equivalent ethylene-glycol coolant per original manual p.253. This original equipment is NOT automatically interchangeable with later Toyota Super Long Life coolant recommendations.
- **Fuel:** 87 AKI or higher, 13.2 US gallon tank (manual p.252).
- **Automatic transaxle:** Toyota Type T-IV ATF, up to 3.0 L drain/refill; actual transaxle model needs identification, not inferred from automatic selector (manual p.254).

## Chassis / brakes / steering
- Brake fluid DOT 3, manual p.254.
- Power steering fluid DEXRON II or III ATF, manual p.254.
- Wheel lug nuts 103 N·m / 76 lb-ft, manual p.255.
- Factory-listed tire sizes P185/65R15 and P195/65R15, both listed at 30 psi cold, manual p.255. **Verify actual tire/door placard**.
- Inspect corrosion at load-bearing mounting points and brake pipes in regions using winter road salt.

## Electrical / lighting
- Verify battery group/terminals and starting/charging health on car; do not order by guessed trim alone.
- U.S. owner's manual pp.76–77 describes reduced-brightness daytime running lights when engine running, parking brake released and headlamp switch OFF. That matters before fitting LEDs; duty cycle / electrical design must be researched and lamp photometrics verified.
- Bulb sizes, configurations and legality to be documented from manual bulb table and actual lamp assembly before upgrades.
