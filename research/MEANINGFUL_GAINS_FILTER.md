# Meaningful-gains filter — corrected evidence treatment (2026-10-25)

**Correction:** Previous filter incorrectly conflated *no validated Corolla-specific result* with *no engineering benefit*, and called potentially useful categories excluded prematurely. This revised document supersedes those exclusion judgments.

**Healthy OEM baseline remains mandatory.** The standard is incremental function, quality, performance or reliability compared with fully functional factory-spec equipment—not recovery from worn parts. Benefits must be weighted by cost, installation burden and tradeoffs. No candidate is purchase-approved merely because mechanism is plausible.

## Evidence classification

- **Demonstrated:** quantified, reproducible comparison of the same vehicle or defensible matched configuration against a healthy stock baseline.
- **Documented mechanism, magnitude unverified:** design introduces a real physical change (e.g. stiffer mount, reduced hose expansion, greater rear roll stiffness); benefit to the driver's desired outcome is unmeasured.
- **Plausible hypothesis:** theory or marketing without an adequate component-specific comparison.
- **Situational advantage:** benefit exists only under a relevant duty cycle (track brake heat, towing ATF temperature, boosted tuning) or a known problem addressed by factory revision.
- **Not supported as upgrade:** stock replacement with no established gain over healthy stock, or wrong-application part.

## Restored to active research, not automatically approved for purchase

| Category | Credible incremental benefit to investigate | Caveats and required test | DIY scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stainless braided brake hoses | Lower pressure expansion, altered pedal travel/feel and modulation | Show healthy-rubber comparison on matching hydraulic system; DOT-compliant fit and proper bleeding | Moderate |
| Front strut tower brace | Reduced local body/strut tower deflection | Structural/steering response measurement; many effects small or setup-specific | Easy–moderate |
| Polyurethane bushings | Reduced elastic bushing movement / altered toe/camber compliance | Geometry and NVH comparison, correct location and installation | Moderate–advanced depending on bushing |
| Stiffer engine/transaxle mounts | Reduced movement under applied torque | Movement measurements, vibration tradeoff; specific mount may require more than moderate work | Moderate where accessible |
| Corolla MagnaFlow 15807 cat-back | Manufacturer claims reduced restriction and dyno-tested power; stainless construction, different sound | Obtain actual comparable dyno curves, tested engine/gearbox, size of gain and price; installation *requires pipe cutting*, so not a simple bolt-on | Moderate–advanced; exhaust cutting |
| Short-ram / CAI intake | Flow restriction change, intake noise, possible power response | Same-car independent dyno, AFR/MAF calibration and heat soak/water exposure against stock airbox | Easy–moderate for verified kit |
| Closed-system oil catch can | Separates some crankcase oil droplets from intake stream | Mass collected, effect on deposit formation, adverse cold-weather freeze/emissions risks | Moderate |
| Premium oil filter | Different filtration efficiency/capacity and valve architecture | Matched ISO test at relevant particle sizes, bypass setting and OEM seal, no extrapolated engine-life claims | Easy |
| Premium engine air filter | Flow/filtration restriction tradeoff | Same filter test method and dust capacity; power not established by flow rate alone | Easy |

## Retained with context

- **Progress 62.2150 24mm rear bar:** manufacturer-supported mechanical roll stiffness change. Higher grip/lap-time performance remains untested; oversteer possible. Moderate DIY.
- **Tires and measurably lighter wheels:** specify exact healthy OE baseline, compare quantitative product test/mass; install easy or via tire shop.
- **High-temperature pad/brake fluid:** benefit mainly repeated thermal demands, not ordinary first-stop distance. Moderate DIY.
- **Auxiliary ATF cooler:** benefit for actual high ATF load, not automatic longevity with healthy stock thermal control. Moderate–advanced plumbing; do not assume need.
- **Oil-pressure/temperature instrumentation:** demonstrable additional information, not inherent power/reliability gain. Moderate.
- **Intake heat management:** requires controlled IAT tests against both existing installed intake and healthy OEM airbox.
- **Factory gasket 17171-22060:** known application engineering revision to research; healthy old gasket replacement is not automatically economically justified.

## Withhold upgrade endorsement, not reject benefit possibility

KYB Excel-G stock-replacement struts, premium blank rotors, exotic spark plugs, silicone coolant hoses, PCV parts: specific performance/quality advantage over **healthy OEM equivalent** not yet established. Treat as ordinary replacement or an open testable hypothesis; a product-specific claim can change that.

Loose Eibach springs, internal transmission kits, engine internals, turbo/supercharger, major swap and other advanced specialist work are **outside current easy/moderate install cutoff**, not dismissed for lack of benefit.

**Research disposition:** Preserve all 148 original entries and open technical reviews. Never translate absence of evidence into evidence of no benefit. Do not promote specific products to confirmed benefits without comparative data.
