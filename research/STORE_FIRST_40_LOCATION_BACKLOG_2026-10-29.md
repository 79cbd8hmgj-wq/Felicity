## Product proof priorities

1. **Diagnose front banging and inspect structure/wheels first.** Record actual wheel sizes, offsets, weights, all four tire sizes/load indices/pressures, suspension travel and safe condition. This gates dynamic tests, not desk research.
2. **Early-build documentation:** correct June 2003 Toyota repair manual/EWD/EPC outputs. Inspect actual 9006 lamp, outlet wiring, seat sensors, rear bar and filter cavity; do not promote October-manual data to June proof.
3. **SPC camber conflict:** actual front strut bolt diameter; current manufacturer application confirmation; Eibach rear label/15mm discrepancy; factory fastening torque and clamping requirements. No ordering until resolved.
4. **Low-cost functionality:** branch-specific SCPDC30-RP1/C0550 quotes; charger operating temperature, cable and socket retention; convex-mirror sightline/adhesion winter trial. These remain modest new capabilities, not major mechanical changes.
5. **HVAC:** 6026C seal/dimensions and pressure-drop/airflow tests versus clean OE filter; independent Corolla defog results. HEPA particle media differs from carbon gas adsorption. 6055C later-generation control is rejected.
6. **Seat heating:** 628-040 actual pad dimensions and non-cut fit, early-seat SRS/sensor location, verified run-only load budget, supplied fuse/relay/tap contents, switch mounting and full materials quote. Reconcile 63W/3.9A fields rather than compute a fictional rating.
7. **Ride/handling:** current KONI availability and complete axle set, mounting/stroke/valving; BTR77 fitting sheet/fasteners and OE bar; polyurethane stiffness/lubrication/noise durability versus new Toyota rubber; independent pothole/rough-road comparisons. Maker's one-hour BTR estimate is not driveway labor proof.
8. **Acoustics/thermal:** dominant road-noise paths, measured selective CLD placement, absorber thickness/moisture constraints, clips/vapor barriers; 050110 package-area conflict; speaker baffle cavity/bass/water-path comparison. No generic dB estimate.
9. **Braking:** ACT923A bracket/clip countermeasure match, wet/cold/fade comparison versus healthy OE; coated caliper/drum durability versus original; current full axle kit/fluid/hardware cost. Camera and pad catalog reviews must not substitute for controlled vehicle tests.
10. **Transmission/steering:** A245E temperature/return-flow evidence before cooler/thermostat design; correct hose ratings and brackets; 20-0038F vs SF and pack quantity, 190/200µm documentation conflict, pressure drop and return hose diameter. No pressure-line filter insertion or assumed T-IV improvement.
11. **Tires and OEM salvage:** early placard, compatible sound 15-inch wheels with correct lug seats; current touring/all-weather/winter SKU tests in relevant size and installed pricing. Dealer brand logos and old government tire prices are not retail quotes. No actual local donor verified.
12. **Powertrain/aero/electrical efficiency:** complete independent usable-rpm response/loss tests, not cone-intake peak claims; inspect current cone and cable throttle. No accepted aerodynamic, brushless blower/alternator, or low-friction bearing retrofit was authenticated through local catalogs in this pass. Those categories remain unexhausted and require electrical/mechanical drawings, not universal labels.
