## Engineering caution and next gap
- Philips promotional brightness claims use **legal minima**, so do not compute them as +100%/+150% compared to a healthy 2004 Corolla lamp assembly. The Philips 9006XVB2 is not lower wattage than healthy stock.
- Denso IK16TT appears in manufacturer catalogs for a related 1ZZ-FE **global Altis** but the source baseline is copper K16PR-U11; it does not rebut that US 2004 Corolla already had platinum/iridium long-life SK16R11.
- FRAM Fresh Breeze technology is real, but no matching 2004 Corolla carbon SKU established this pass. Filter-house dimensions and actual fan performance remain to be compared.
- Work further on approved US-spec halogen photometric testing and matching 2004 Corolla carbon cabin-filter model; avoid claiming added MPG or horsepower from these parts.

**Result:** no new proven healthy-OEM engine-power gain from premium plugs/filters. Lighting technology has useful measurable parameters, but **the exact healthy-stock Corolla comparison is still absent**.
