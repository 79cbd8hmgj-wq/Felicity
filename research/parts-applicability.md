## Research methodology

Verify each replacement against the June 2003 build date, LE trim, 1ZZ-FE engine and A245E transaxle. Record original Toyota part number, any supersession, assembly compatibility, source, certainty and evidence. Avoid treating a generic 2004 Corolla product listing as exact fitment confirmation.

Prioritize automatic-transmission service components, cooling, lubrication, ignition, brakes, charging, suspension and daytime-running-light wiring.
