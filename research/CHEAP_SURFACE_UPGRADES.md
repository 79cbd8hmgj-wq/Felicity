**Scope discipline:** The FRAM cabin filter and OBD monitor are already researched; do not count a second vendor as a new upgrade. This pass found **surface-treatment functionality** but **no proven fuel, electrical or mechanical efficiency gain** over healthy OEM equipment. Continue searching for genuinely new affordable efficiency mechanisms rather than inflating candidate counts.
