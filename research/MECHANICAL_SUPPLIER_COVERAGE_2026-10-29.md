## Retrofit and material limits

PowerGrip has a documented thermal-memory mechanism and other-manufacturer production use. That supports a real technology, not superiority to Toyota spring clamps. Current Gates SB41 example covers38–44mm hose OD, but no Corolla clamp size is assigned. SB34 differs between historical Freightliner and current maker tables; use the current application-specific selection, not a nominal hose-ID guess. Heat application, disposable removal, plastic outlet condition and future roadside servicing belong in the comparison. Full current Gates PDF retrieval failed; a current specification page and the historical Freightliner document were read. No complete DIY price is established.

ECD-resistant EPDM is a credible material option, but original Toyota hose ECD resistance is unverified. No basis exists to count standard SAE-rated replacement as an incremental improvement. SKF testing compares industrial sleeve generations, not Toyota crankshafts; retain it as a conditional worn-counterface solution with unassigned size and cost. The owner clamp thread supplies useful negative evidence: the Corolla intervention was only proposed, and the decade-long success belongs to another vehicle. No Level B promotion.
